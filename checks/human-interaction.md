# SUS Checks — Human Interaction

Is your AI use sustainable *for you*, for your skills, judgment, and learning?
This check-list is meant to help you find out; run it top to bottom, with every
item pass/fail. **Any fail motivates action and does not constitute a crisis.**
See the [guidelines](../guidelines/) for how to take action to improve your AI
tool use.

---

**H1 — No-AI baseline.** I can still do this task, at acceptable quality, without
AI. *(If not then the AI tool is not assisting, it is replacing. Pick one component
of the task to do unassisted this week.)*

**H2 — Verified the "load-bearing" parts.** Every claim, number, citation, or result
that this work depends on was checked against a source other than the AI tool that
produced it.

**H3 — Judgment stayed human.** The decisions that carry responsibility were made
by me (e.g. what to do, what to recommend, what to sign). The AI tool assisted in
drafting, but I decided.

**H4 — I can explain this work.** I can explain any part of the finished work
without the transcript open. *(If not: I accepted output I do not understand.)*

**H5 — Skill trajectory.** In the area this task belongs to, my own skill is growing
or holding, not declining. *(Honest test: could I have done this task two years ago?
Can I still?)*

**H6 — Right tool for the cognitive work.** I used the AI tool for drudgery it is
good at, not for the thinking I am supposed to be doing myself.

**H7 — No ghost-editing of my own understanding.** Where AI helped me learn
something, the practice or retrieval happened with guardrails (I attempted before I
used the AI tool), not by consumption of AI-generated answers.

**H8 — Dependency awareness.** I know which parts of my workflow I would lose if the
AI tool were unavailable tomorrow. I have at least one non-AI fallback, recently
tested, for each part of my workflow that depends on AI tools.

**H9 — Disclosure of AI use.** Where my AI tool use matters to the audience of the
work (co-author, employer, client, student, reader), it is disclosed or disclosed-able
without reputational harm.

**H10 — Re-run scheduled.** These checks are re-run on a schedule (at least every
6 months) by me with some automation (AI or otherwise), which also flags material
changes when they surface.
